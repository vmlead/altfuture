The Alternate Future Summit Seminar 2020

Come and participate in a hackathon to ‘Solve global challenges’ and ‘define the future’. The summit is an initiative designed to encourage developers, start-ups, technologists, and corporate management to unite and develop solutions that'll help large scale recovery and define future.
Join us from August 7 - 14 (12 pm CET)

Problem Statements to address in Hackathon

    • 1. PDS (Public distribution system)

    • Public distribution system for poor and needy. This could be around food, medicines or any other basic essential supplies

    • 2. Recognising the frontline warriors

    • Recognising the frontline warriors that have emerged in the shape of healthcare workers, policemen, government officials and others

    • 3. Social & Political Cohesion

    • It is about supporting quarantined or self-isolated people to remain safe and sane. It also addresses and scale a range of social and political initiatives to support citizens as such, communities, democracy, EU values and fundamental rights.

    • 4. Virtual education platforms/gaming education platforms

    • Impact on Education and new solutions aiming at Learning-Continuity. Solutions for making digital education easier, engaging and equal for all.

    • 5. Setting up for Post Covid Recovery Jobs/Financial Systems/Al/Healthcare

    • Solutions aimed at near term recovery from the impact of COVID19 in a post crisis era and the new normal
